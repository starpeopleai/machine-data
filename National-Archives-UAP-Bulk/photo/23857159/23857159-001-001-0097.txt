On 9 February 1954 at 2105 hours three crewembers of a 8-26 from the 1lth
Tactical Reconnaissance Squadron observed an unusual object in the vicinity of
their aircraft. The observation was made while the aircraft was located at
IB 0768, on a magnetic heading of 104 degrees, at an altitude of 9,000 feet,
and at a speed of 2h0 MPH.
The object was first seen as a glowing white object ascending from the two
o'clock position to an estimated altitude of 20,000 feet and almost directly
above the aircraft.
This object then began a gradual descent to an estimated
altitude of 1l,000 feet at which time it appeared to increase in size, change
from a white to a greenish color, and disappear at the ten o'clock position.
When first seen the object was approximately the size of a dime and increased
in size until it was approximately the size of a silver dollar at the time it
disappeared,
No definite conclusions could be made as to the nature of the object due
to limited information available for analysis, however, a flare or spent tracer
seems to be the most reasonable possibility.
