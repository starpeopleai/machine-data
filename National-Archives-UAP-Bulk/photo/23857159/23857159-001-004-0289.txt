REPORT OF THE INVESTIGATING COMMITTEE OF THE C.I.0.V.I. (INVESTIGATING CENTER OF
UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS) ON THE CASE WHICH OCCURRED MARCH-APRIL 1957
The C.I.O.V.I. thinks it necessary to point out first the uncertainty of the
date, due to the fact that the observer sent the letter many months after, on account
of having only then heard our radio-audition on the subject of the OVIS.
In his letter and observation report he stresses the fact that he only saw the
object for a few seconds (3 or 4).
This justifies the scarcity of the data, which
makes it difficult to study the case.
As to the type of the object, we want to point out that there is a previous ex-
perience proven) in Brazil in 1957•
We also have reference to an object seen in
Solis (Uruguay) in the year 1958, for 3 days running, around 11 A.M. The object was
different in color but with a similar shape.
It is characteristic of some OVIS that they disappear suddenly; such a thing
occurred in this case.
As the observer states, the sun had already set, but even so the object was very
bright, which indicates it had its own light.
Everything indicates we are in the
presence of an Unidentified Object.
