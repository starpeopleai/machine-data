1. Appearance of unidentified Lying object in the KUIDJA area
F1 29
In Jul 59 at approximately 2100 hours ane evening,
an unidentified flying
object had been seen in the KULDJA area.
It resembled a ball of fire, which
burnt with an intense orange glow.
The object had appeared to the west of
the town and had disappeared over the mountains to the north.
One casual
observer had formed no estimate of its height, but had noticed that it had been
moving at a great speed and had remained in sight for some two minutes.
This phenomenon had caused quite a stir of public interest in KULDJA, as
many people had seen it, and it had remained the main subject of conversation
for several days.
There had been much idle speculation among the inhabitants
of KULDJA concerning the nature and origin of this object but the press and
radio had made no reference to it:

Appearance of nocturnal flashes in the KUIDJA area
1959
In March or April of 1959 for about two months, flashes had been seen at
night rising from the airfield in KULDJA.
They were not fireworks.
reaching certain heights they had formed themselves into small red balls,
floated away in all directions and disappeared. They had always been seen
at about 11 pm to 12 midnight, but had not appeared every night. No
explosions nad been neard.
On two occasions in about September or October of 1959, two very large
flashes had been seen at about 10 pm emanating from some point over the
Soviet border, north-west of KULDJA.
A large red flash would be seen at
first and then a huge reddish cloud, resembling a mishroom, would appear.
This cloud had gradually changed from a red to a dull grey colour and then
slowly disintegrated.
After the cloud formed it would remain in view from
ten to fifteen minutes.
No explosions nad been neard on either occasion,
the second of which took place a week after the first.
