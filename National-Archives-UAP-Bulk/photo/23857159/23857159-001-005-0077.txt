ations moving in a southwesterly direction toward the Colony of Hong Kong.

060 degrees and the ground speed was 45 mph.

The second formation was observed at 1549Z at an altitude of 5000 ft., a range

It traveled a similar course,
parallel to that of the first form
ation, for a distance of 35 miles.
