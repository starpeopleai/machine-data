REPORT OF THE INVESTIGATING COMMITTEE OF THE C.I.O.V.I. (INVESTIGATING CENTER OF
UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS) ON THE CASE WHICH OCCURRED 26 JUNE 1958
There are no doubts that what Mrs. Amelia Lacandegui de Lescumes saw was an Un-
identified Flying Object.
The fact that only Mrs. Lescumes was the only one that saw the Flying Object is
because it is a milking farm and the men sleep during the day and start work after
2 A.M.
But the fact that she is the only witness does not make her story less authentic,
as the conversation we had with this lady proved her to be a serious person of wide
culture and, as she stated, has been very interested in OVI problems which she has
studied during her voyages to Europe and the U.S.A.
It is to be pointed out that a new form of OVI is added to the large variety
already known.
This new variety also surprised Ing. Jao Martins (distinguished
Brazilian investigator in such matters).
Another particular characteristic in this case was a loud noise (high frequency
vibration) that stunned the lady and frightened the chickens that rushed into the
house. As to the smell that Mrs. Lescumes noticed, there are previous experiences
on record, such as the case of Sonny Desvergers, studied by Edward Ruppelt (Chief of
the Project BLUE BOOK, official organ of the U. S. Air Force for the study of Un-
identified Flying Objects).
The smell of sulphur that the lady smelled came from ozone gas.
You can obtain ozone
gas by passing air between two objects highly electrically
charged (in our case, OVI - earth).
By what the lady says we gather that the Object was hidden behind some trees that
border a small stream about 200 meters away.
Many are the things that indicate that
these objects are refuelled with sweet water.
By all this, the C.l.O.V.I. reiterates that it was an Unidentified Flying Object
that in some way has an entirely new aspect, such as its formation.
