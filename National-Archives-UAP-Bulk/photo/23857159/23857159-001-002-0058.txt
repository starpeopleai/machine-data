At 17132, 25 January 1956, site 15(3046N - 12840) of the 610th AC&N
Squadron reported an unidentified track in the Yankee Foxtrot area.
The track
was at 20,000 feet, heading southwest, range 150 nautical miles at a speed of
1600 knots.
The track was not flashed because of its range and because it was outbound.
There was consequently no fighter action taken.
The track appeared to be a normal target. It was sharp and bright with no
evidence of fuzziness.
