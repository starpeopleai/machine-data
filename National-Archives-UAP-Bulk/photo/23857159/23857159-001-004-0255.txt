Quoted herewith is extract of letter from Father Risacker who resides
at Kisalon, Mindanao (8°20'N - 124058'E), to Father Hennessey, Director of the
Manila Observatory, Baguio.
"At about 8:15 (May 17) p.m. I stood on the little platform at the head
of the back stairs to admire the starry heavens and behold, there came out of
the western sky, due west in the ecliptic (zodiac), southern part, a very bright,
silvery bluish round body giving off sharp rays all around, more brilliant than
Venus. And this was its apparent course:
Perhaps 40° altitude
Disappeared from my view behind
Paliapan Mt. Head.
"There was no sign of a trail or light streak behind. It moved away
very fast but it seemed to stop or linger in the small up curve and then dash
down again. The mountain head, perhaps 1,200 feet above our plain, is only 2
‹ilometers directly west, so it cut off sight completely. The direction was SW-
NE. If it is true that such phenomena are within 50 miles I thought many would
notice it. It seems to me a meteor could not follow such a course. The whole
phenomenon was over in more or less a minute."
