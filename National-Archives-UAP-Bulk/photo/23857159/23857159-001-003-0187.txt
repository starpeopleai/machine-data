ported in to S2 by telephone at 0500,about 5 - 7 minutes later Johnson cal-

something flying - could not see any object. It was a clear night - moon-
light - could see the stars - Johnson told me about the bright light and
pointed the vapor out to me.
I thought it was some kind of jet because
it left a trail or stream of white smoke. It was just then, a few seconds
before the cloud passed over it or it passed behind, the cloud. I would say
it was about 20° - about 12 inches in length at arms length. At an angle

or from my right to the left. I shot the angle with the lensatic compass
as about 240
- 250° - I don't remember exactly - notes should be in S2

plane.
