8, In an opinion dated 14 February 1949, the Attorney
General approved the application of section 207 of the
Legislative Reorganization Act to cases involving decisions
of the discharge review board, notwithstanding the statutory
provision that findings of the discharge review board were
"to be final subject only to review by the Secretary of War
or the Secretary of the Navy, respectively" (41 Ops Att'y
Gen, Op. No, 1). Discharge review boards were authorized by
section 301 of the Servicemen's Readjustment Act of 1944
(38 U.S.C. 693h) and those boards were granted authority to
change, correct, or modify any discharge or dismissal except
a discharge or dismissal by reason of the sentence of a general
court-martial. In his opinion, the Attorney General held
that while an individual whose case had been considered by a
discharge review board could not “as of right" invoke the
procedures set up by section 207 of the Legislative Reorgan-
ization Act, "there does not appear to be any sound reason
for an interpretation so narrowing the terms of the latter
section as to deny to the respective secretaries the authority
to invoke that section ‘where in their judgment such action
48 necessary to correct an error or to remove an injustice.'"
