lll Stresses and Configurations
of Natural-Shaped Balloons

Justin H. Smalley
Applied Science Division
Litton Systems, Inc.

Abstract

The equations derived in the early work by the University of Minnesota for the
stresses in free balloons have been verified. The coordinates for natural-shaped
balloons as determined by the University on an analog computer have been refined
by digital computer techniques. In addition, a larger class of balloon shapes have
been computed, including larger sigma values, both super- and sub-pressures, non-
zero circumferential stresses, and other than ''flat-top'' balloons. The meridional

stresses have also been investigated.
