X Bal-Ast Daylight Tracking System
Murk Bottema
The Johns Hopkins University
Abstract
In the Bal-Ast program for infrared planetary spectroscopy off-set suntracking
is used for coarse orientation of the spectrometer-telescope, so as to keep the
planet in the field of the telescope for the duration of a 1-2 hour observation period.
The planet's image is then transferred to the entrance slit of the spectrometer by
means of an optical relay, controlled by a startracker. A final tracking accuracy of
5-10 arcseconds is achieved. This paper describes details of the suntracking sys-
tem and its performance during a May 1963 flight.
