Abstract
A resume of the present load/altitude capabilities of plastic balloons, including
taped and tapeless polyethylene, Mylar-scrim, superpressure, and air-launched, is
presented. Objectives of present research in balloon technology are described:
namely, extensions of present altitude and load limits through material and design
studies, increased reliability, longer duration and reduced costs of very heavy-
load balloons.
