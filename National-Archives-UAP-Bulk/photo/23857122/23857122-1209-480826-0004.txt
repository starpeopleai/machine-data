127 Blair Street
Ithaca, New York
May 15, 1952
To Whom It May Concern:
This is to comply with a request made in the April 7th issue of
Life Magazine in an article relative to Flying Saucers, etc. which
asked that any persons seeing such oddities notify their nearest Air
Force installation - which, in this case, is yours.
On the evening of Thursday, April 3, my husband who is a Crew Chief
at Robinson Airlines and has been engaged actively in aeronautics for
12 years, part of which was with the AAF, and a friend who is a school
teacher and I were traveling in an automobile north along the west
shore of Cayuga Lake when at a point about 7 miles from the city of
Ithaca at 9:10 PM we saw a "Fireball" cross Cayuga Lake in an arc
traveling very rapidly from Northeast to Northwest. It was a sizable
ball of fire, green in color with green flames streaking out.
This information was not forwarded sooner because I had not read
the article and noted the request until today.
Very sincerely yours,
Signed: Mrs. Walter Lee Ott
