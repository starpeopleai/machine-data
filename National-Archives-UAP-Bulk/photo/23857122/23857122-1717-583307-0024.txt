At approximately 2315 MST on 5 August 1953, I noticed an unidentified
object northeast of Black Hawk, South Dakota. It appeared six or seven
times while I was watching it and through the binoculars it looked like
a basketball. It had various colors and a lot of speed. It would
appear and disappear and would never appear in the same place. When
the last jet appeared in the vicinity, it disappeared and I never saw it
again.
Olaf Aldren
