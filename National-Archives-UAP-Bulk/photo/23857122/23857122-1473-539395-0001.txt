At approximately 1825 EST, 11 March 1953, the following information was received
by this station (P-49) from the GOC Post at Watertown, New York:
GOC post at Watertown, New York notified this station that an unidentified fly-
ing object was observed approximately one (1) mile West of their post located 43 de-
grees 58 minutes N, 75 degrees 5h minutes W, at approximately 1810 EST, 11 March 1953.
This object was observed by twelve (12) persons of the GOC, and was reported as oval
in shape, with two (2) fairly large shafts, glowing like a star, white, orange, blue,
and green in color, traveling at jet speed, at an altitude of approximately 20,000 to
25,000 feet.
Color was later given as orange,
No sound was audible. Object was
observed for approximately two hours and 10 minutes.
Weather in that vicinity was 5,000 thinly overcast, with winds 20 to 25 knots.
The authenticity of this report seems fairly genuine since it was observed by
twelve persons of the GOC for approximately two hours.
This object was not picked up on the radar at this site at any time.
