At 0830 EST, 1 October 1953, Mr. Earnest L. Kaumitz, 1013 Ketchum St., Bay City,
Michigan observed, visually, an unknown flying object approximately 5 miles south of
Harrisville, Michigan.
Mr. Kaumitz describes the object that he saw as being round,
highly reflective, and in a turn when he observed it.
The object appeared to be
turning to a heading of 225 degrees at an altitude of about twenty degrees above the
horizon. Observer was unable to detect any noise or exhaust trail of any type. Object
was observed for approximately 3 to 4 seconds before being lost from sight.
Two flights plus one tow ship of F-86 type aircraft were airborn in the immediate
area at 20,000 feet firing gunnery.
The local radar station reports no contact except
with the gunnery flights.
Weather at the time of sighting was clear with twenty to thirty miles visability.
Mr. Kaumitz reported a similar sighting on 28 September 1953 from a location
l½ miles distant to the location of this sighting.
