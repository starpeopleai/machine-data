The Testimony of Mr. V. I. Black Regarding Unidenti-
fied Citing
QUESTION: Where was the location of your point of observation?
ANSWER:
I was at that time on my own property which I was clearing
for a house to be built. This property is located on the
south side of Otter Creek on Otter Point. Otter Point is
almost four miles north of sau Gallie, Florida. This prop-
erty is on US Highway 1, east side and west bank of the
Indian River.
QUESTION: What was the distance, direction, and altitude of the craft?
ANSWER:
I had walked to my car and was facing approximately south.
I glanced to the right and upward. This unidentified ob-
ject came into my line of view over my right shoulder. It
appeared to be lower than 500 feet, and the bottom outline
was reflected in the neon lights of the Westchester Motor
Court which lies directly west of the position described,
about 250 to 350 feet away. I estimated the speed to be
500 miles an hour or excess.

QUESTION:
ANSWER:
This was the evening of 4 January 1953. The atmospheric
condition was exceptionally clear.
It was quite cold, the
temperature being about 500 F. There was little or no wind;
in fact, it was calm.
Would you please state what background and knowledge you
have with regard to aircraft.
I have, over the past six years, done a great deal of travel
by airplane. In fact, while living in Puerto Rico it was
my main mode of transportation. I have flown at the rate
of 20,000 to 30,000 miles a year. Too, I have been on Patrick
over one and one-half years during which time I have observed
the movement of aircraft to and from the Base. I have also
been at Eglin Air Force Base where I observed B-36's daily.
I do not fly any type of aircraft myself.
"I certify that to the best of my knowledge, the above is true and
correct."
