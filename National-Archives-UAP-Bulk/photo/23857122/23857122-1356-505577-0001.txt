1. This report contains information of the sighting of a luminous object from
Sampson Air Force Base, New York at 1820 hours, 26 January 1953.
20
A/2C Salzberg, Sampson AFB, reported in letter form the visual observation
of a luminous object about 180
feet long and 12 feet wide.
The object traveled
through the sky from east to west at an altitude estimated to be between 7,000
Both the front and the rear of the object seemed to have a hazy
appearance, but the lengthy body was clearly outlined. The object seemed to
rapidly gain altitude then fade away. A high-pitched hum, similar to that of an
electrical motor, was heard coming from the direction of the object.
After, the
object disappeared from sight the hum was heard for a few seconds then the sound
abruptly ceased.
3. The second witness A/3C John F. Brostoski, also of Sampson AFB, stated in letter
form that he had been called from his quarters by A/2C Salzberg. A/3C Brostoski
heard the high-pitched hum, however did not see the object.
Further investigation revealed that the object apparently traveled from east
to west throughout an arc of 70 to 80 degrees in a straight shallow climb.
Theodolite reading taken later determined the angle of elevation at initial sight-
ing to be approximately 009 degrees.
At the end of the sighting the arc was
estimated to be 048 degrees.
The duration of the sighting was approximately one
minute.
