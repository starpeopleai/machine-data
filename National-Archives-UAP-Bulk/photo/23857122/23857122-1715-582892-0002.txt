SECOND REPORT
At approximately 06118, on 6 August 1953, an unidentified object was seen
6-12 miles NNW of Bismark, North Dakota.
Object was first sighted by S/Sgt
Wesley Harry and 2/Lt Charles W. Koontz, and was verified by four other obser-
vers, whose statements accompany this report. Object was round and changed
colors from white, to orange, to red, to green. Object seemed to send out
short flashes of light when maneuvering. Speed was slow with steady movement,
both vertically and laterally. No sound was audible. The direction the object
approached from is unknown.
The object disappeared at approximately 0850%.
The object was observed from the top of the building housing the Bismark Filter
Center, and from the CAA tower at Bismark Municipal Airport, both located in
Burleigh County, North Dakota.
Motion was detected by lining object up with
stationary objects. Sky was clear; surface winds light from northwest; winds
aloft reported by CAA to be from 330° at 25 knots. We were unable to obtain
any photos as requested by the ADDC at Rapid City, South Dakota, which was
first notified of the sighting at approximately 06152. The grid location of
the original sighting was EB 1754.
The object moved easterly and was last re-
ported at EB 2356. This unidentified object was observed by the following:
2/It C. W. Koontz
S/Sgt W. N. Harry
