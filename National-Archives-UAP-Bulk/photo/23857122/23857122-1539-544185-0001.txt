Unidentified, unconventional flying objects were sighted by Weldon W.
Watson, 112 William Street, East Prairie, Missouri, 16 April 1953. Objects
were also observed by Mrs. Watson, Mrs. Stella Shortz and a fourth unidentified
person.
Objects were observed for 25 minutes between 1510 and 1535 hours (Central
Standard Time) at estimated 40,000 feet.
Position of objects were SE to ESE
of observers and appeared to be in trail of USAF B-36. (distance in trail
unknown). By comparison with B-36 objects were circular and approximately
230' in diameter and silver in color.
No visible propulsion system was
observed and no exhaust gases or sound was evident.

After making a sharp turn to the left it was joined by three additional
similar objects. The four objects then completed a tight 360° maneuver coming
out abreast and continuing on the original heading, SE, (in trail of B-36)
at a greatly increased speed. The four objects disappeared parallel to the
trail of the B-36.
Soon after the original four disappeared, three more were seen to be
maneuvering to the ESE of observers.
For a ten minute period one of the objects
remained motionless while the remaining two were in motion in the general
vicinity. Mr. Watson further reported observing what seemed to be alternate
flashing signals; but were not in Morse Code.
