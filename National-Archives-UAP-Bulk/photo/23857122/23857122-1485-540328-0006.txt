I, 1st Lt William F. Farley, A0 715 752, make the following statement.
At 2000Z 7 February 1953 while on duty as controllar in 705th AC&W
Squadron radar operations the Naknek Detachment of the 64th Fighter-
Interceptor Squadron called me and said that King Salmon Tower informed
them that they could see an unusual light in the sky.
Tower requested
the information be passed to radar operations. we could see nothing unusual
on the radar scope.
On 9 February I went to King Salmon Airport and interviewed the per-
sonnel who saw the light.
James N. Burrus who was on duty in the Control
Tower was the first to see the light.
We took a bearing from land marks
he noted during the sighting and got a magnetie bearing, of 250° from the
Control Tower.
