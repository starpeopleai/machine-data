Statement of Mrs O.W. Vannoy, 419 Finfrock, Pasadena, Texas
On 23 March 1953 at approximately 0400Z I sighted an object
in the sky over Pasadena.
The object was yellow or amber in color
very similar to star in appearance except that it was much
brighter. I observed the object circling over Pasadena at various
speeds at an undetermined height. It seemed to speed up and
and slow down at varying intervals of time in between each one.
It continued to circle the town for about 20 minutes then dis-
appeared in a nn a northwest direction. There was no sound
and it would be very hard to determine the altitude. It also
appeared to come closer to the ground and then rise again.
appeared to be a grayish behind the object although it could
have been an optical illusion.
Mo. D. W. Vannay
Mrs O.W. Vannoy
419 Finfrock
Pasadena, Texas
Housewife
