MEDITERRANEAN SEA

Third Officer Frank Kaminski, of the American S. S. Atlantic
Seaman, Capt. Martin E. Dahl, Master, reports that while en route ©
to Port Said at 1955 G. M. T., October 18, 1952, in lat. 35°57’ N., |
lon. 15°48’ E., he observed a comet-like streak which appeared in
the north at an altitude of 35°, which moved in a westward and
downward direction for about 2 or 3 seconds when at a bearing :
of 845° it exploded with a blinding flash which illuminated the
entire area for about a second. A trail was visible in the path
of the object to where it exploded, then curved upward and back
to the eastward in a hook-shaped formation of rust-colored smoke.
This trail was visible for at least 10 minutes before it disappeared |
completely.

* A similar observation was made by Mr. Kaminski on the night
of November 16 in South Suez Bay, but was bright orange in |
color and cigar shaped. It was headed southward at an altitude |
of 15° and was visible about 5 seconds.
