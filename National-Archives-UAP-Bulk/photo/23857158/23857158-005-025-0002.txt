1. Attached as Part II is a report in accordance with par 7.a. (2) (a),
AFR 200-2, of an object landing near a Fort Holabird installation, Baltimore, Md,
at approxinately 1620Z, 7 July 1955.
2. The object, spherical and about 4 feet in diameter, later reducing to
about 3 feet in diameter, appeared to be blown by a light wind toward SOURCE,
landing approximately 100 feet from him. The object was then blown across the
ground by a gentle breeze to a wire fence, which stopped the object. At this
point, the object began to evaporate or dissolve, without leaving any trace on
surrounding areas or on the fence.
Before the object completely disappeared on
the ground, SOURCE was able to obtain a small portion of the substance of the
object (Incl 1). SOURCE's description of the object, confirmed by other observers
at the scene, was that the object appeared to be a bright, silvery (as shiny
aluminum) mass of tiny bubbles,
each bubble about the size of a pencil eraser.
SOURCE provided a number of small twigs and leaves on which he stated the object
had rested before "disappearing" (Incl 2).
3. A newspaper account of other sightings of similar objects in nearby
areas at the time of the observation reported here is attached as inclosure 3.
4. A number of industrial installations are in the immediate area of this
sighting and processes, particularly those involving the production of soap,
could have generated the object of this report.
5•
SOURCE was commended verbally for his co-operative spirit and told his
report and the materials he obtained would be sent to the proper authorities
for further investigation and evaluation.
