Sun’s Closest Star

Alpha Centauri is famous because it is.
the sun’s closest neighbor in space. Actu-
ally it is a double star, consisting of two
separate orbs, revolving around a common
center of gravity. They have a more distant
and fainter companion, which may be a
little closer than the bright pair. This is
called Proxima.

The distance of the Alpha Centauri. sys-
tem is about 26 million million miles, or 4.3
light years. That is, their light, which travels
186,000 miles every second, takes that many
years to reach us. If, in a scale model, the
period at the end of this sentence repre-
sented the sun, Alpha Centauri would be a
coion five miles away!
