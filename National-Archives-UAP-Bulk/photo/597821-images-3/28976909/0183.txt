Two instances in which private indi-
viduals succeeded in obtaining photo-
graphs of UAQOs within the last few weeks
have come to the attention of the APRO
staff,

On the fourth of March, Charles Mor-
ris, 31, an airplane instructor, obtained
19 feet of moving picture film of a for-
mation of three disc-shaped objects over
Dubugue, Iowa. Morris estimated their
altitude as 20,000 feet, their speed at
about 200 miles per hour. The objects
were saucer-shaped, silvery in color.
Morris gave the film to the Federal gov-
ernment ‘‘for processing and study,’’ the
UPI report stated.

At Grand Blane, Michigan, Joe Perry,
a pizza restaurant owner and amateur
astronomer, shot a few pictures of the

full moon in February. When the color
pictures were developed, one showed,

clearly silhouetted against the lunar disc,
a saucer-like object, radiating a green
tail, and with a dome. Mr. Perry showed
them around his restaurant, to customers
and friends, and one individual said, ‘It’s
a flying saucer.’’ Before long, two FBI
agents dropped in, looked at the pic-
tures, were noncommital when looking
at them, and in Perry’s words, “They
didn’t seem to be interested until I show-
ed this particular one, and then both of
them jumped up.”’ The agents took the
phote showing the saucer, and several
