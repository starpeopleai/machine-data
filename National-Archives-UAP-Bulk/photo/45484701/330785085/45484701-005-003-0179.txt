DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE
HEADQUARTERS UNITED STATES AIR FORCE
WASHINGTON 25, D. C.
6 March 1950
MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD
1. It Colonel Patrick Hayes, OSI District Commander, Maxwell Air
Force Base, telephonically furnished the following advice on the evening
of 3 March 1950:
Mr. S. E. Adaock, Chief of Engineers, Radio Statior
NROL, Knoxville, Tennessee, reported to A. F. Davis,
Chief of the AEC Security Patrol at Oak Ridge, Tennessee,
that he sighted through radar set ANP-7 at 2330 hours,
L March 1950, one unidentified object, altitude 40,000
feet, bearing 3320 from Knoxville. The radar set operated
on 19,000 megacycle frequency. Speed or direction of
object undetermined.
b. At 2000 hours, 2 March 1950 he sighted two unidentified
objects, altitude 80,000 feet, bearing 310° from Knoxville,
rennessee, same frequency. No speed or direction indicated.
c. At 1035 hours, 3 March 1950 he sighted one unidentified
object, altitude 40,000 feet, bearing 3200 from Knoxville,
same frequency, approximate speed 200 miles per hour. No
direction determined, approximate size of C-47 aircraft.
d. Colonel Hayes also advised that Colonel Gasser, Air
Force liaison to NEPA, was advised of this and requested
Wright Patterson AFB to send adequate radar sets to Knox-
ville. According to CIC, Knoxville, the Third Army will
send mobile anti-aircraft radar sets if the AF cannot
provide the radar sets necessary. A. F. Davis contacted
the CO at Smyrna AFB as to any AF aircraft flying in the
vicinity, receiving a negative reply.
e. Mr. Charles White, Chief of the Physics Section of
AEC, Oak Ridge, Tennessee, made an informal request to the
local OSI representative to secure three portable radar
sets from AF sources. The OSI representative advised White
that OSI would process the request only if it were formally
made.
White stated that he desired to obtain approval from
higher AEC authority before making a formal request.
