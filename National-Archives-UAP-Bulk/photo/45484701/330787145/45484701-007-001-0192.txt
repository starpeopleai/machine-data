On 21 July 1952, this office was advised by Mr. PAUL BURKE
that he had sighted an unidentified aerial object while aboard a
United Airlines airplane, east of Sandusky, Ohio over Lake Erie, at
about 11:00 AM on 17 July 1952.
Mr. BURKE described himself as a private pilot since 1929
and the owner of a Buick agency in Brooklyn, !...
