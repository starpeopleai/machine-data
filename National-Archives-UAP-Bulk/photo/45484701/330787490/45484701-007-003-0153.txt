1. SYNOPSIS: An unusual cloud formation with brilliant coloration
was observed North West of SAN JOSE, CALIFORNIA and caused considerable
excitement in the SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA. Weather Bureau officials
claimed the cloud to be an unusual grouping of moisture particles in the
sky which gave vivid prismatic effects.

2. DETAILS: On 2 August 1950, Mr. JOSEPH L. CLOTHEIR, Aircraft
Controller, OAKLAND Airport Service, together with two (2) other
Controllers, of the OAKLAND Airport Service stated at approximately
1040 they noted a set of two (2) cloud formations close together and
in the cloud formations they noticed a number of vivid colors running
from a bright yellow into orange and pastel green running into blue at
at approximately 330° from the OAKLAND Tower. The clouds once appeared
to be very high. By 1100 the phenomena had dispersed and the clouds
turned into the normal cloud formations. The distance appeared to be
approximately 25 miles from the tower in an easterly direction; over
water by the SAN MATEO BRIDGE approximately west and north of SAN JOSE.
