SUMMARY OF INFORMATION
The following report was received at 2018 hours, 23 February 56, by the First
Army Staff Duty Officer, by an individual identifying himself as Bill BANCKERT or
BORCHARDT, of the NY Journal American.
1. A Sgt George PIERCE, further identifying data unknown, but believed to be
a member of the U.S.Army, was walking a dog near his mother's residence on Moffet
Ave., in Bklyn, NY at approximately 2000 hours, 23 Feb 56, Sgt PIERCE was on leave
at the time.
2. Sgt PIERCE heard a noise as from an airplane in trouble and, upon looking
up, saw a round, big, blue flugrescent lighted object in the sky, which went in
the direction of Idlewild Airport, circled the airport, and then left in the direc-
tion of lower New York. Sgt PIERCE described this object as a flying saucer.
3. The caller described Sgt PIERCE as apparently rational and sober and added
that the Sgt was due to go overseas in the near future.
