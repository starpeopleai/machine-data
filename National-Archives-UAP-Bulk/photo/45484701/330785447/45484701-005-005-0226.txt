TWO "FLYING SAUCERS" SEEN BY WACO COUPLE
Two "flying saucers" were reported whisking through the
night sky over Waco late Saturday by Mr. and Mrs. S. L.
SMITH of 908 South Seventeenth.
"The objects looked like two little moons traveling at ter-
rific speed coming from the direction of Temple and heading
north about where Elm Street leaves Waco, " Smith stated.
He and his wife came outside about 10:30 p. m. Saturday just
after the clouds had broken up over Waco. They were looking
at the stars which had been visible only rarely during the
past week, SMITH related.
Suddenly they both saw the greenish objects, about half the
size of a full moon, pass overhead. Mrs. SMITH called to
another couple indoors to come out, but by the time they ar-
rived at the door, the dimly "glowing missiles" had disap-
peared in the north. SMITH estimated it took the "discs"
which remained about the same distance apart from each other,
Asked if he thought the objects could have been meteors,
SMITH said they left no afterglow or trail of fire, and were
not glowing brightly, just a greenish tint. He said the front
ends were not as bright as the rear parts of the dises, and
the circumference lines were not quite straight, as if
might have been spinning.
Meanwhile, the SMITHS spent Saturday and Sunday night watch-
ing for a re-appearance of the objects, but so far have seen
them no more.
