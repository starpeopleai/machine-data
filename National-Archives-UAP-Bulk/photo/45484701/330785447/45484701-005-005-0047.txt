CONFIDENTIAL
On 23 'May 1949, this reporting agent and S/A Koster of
Fourth Army CIC interviewed MRS THEODORE MICHEL, housewife,
1796 Paul Morphy St., New Orleans, La. MRS MICHEL was contact-
ed after this agent ascertained from MRS AUSTIN that MRS
MICHEL had called MRS AUSTIN'S husband on Saturday, 21 May 1949,
to report that she, too, had seen the flying disk.
However,
MRS MICHEL sighted
the disk on the day previous to MRS AUSTIN.
The following information was obtained from MRS MICHEL relative
to the sighted object:
a. Date of sighting: Wednesday, 18 May 1949.
b. Time of sighting: 1630 to 1645 hours.
Where sighted:
The observer was at her home at 1756
Paul Morphy St., New Orleans, La., at the time of sighting.
The object appeared to be on the outskirts of the city in the
vicinity of Lake Pontchartrain, which is approximately two (2)
miles from MRS MICHEL'S home, in a western direction.
At the
time of sighting, MRS MICHEL was watering her lawn and working
in the garden.
d. Number of objects: MRS MICHEL sighted only one (1)
object.
No other craft of any type were in the sky within her
line of vision.
Distance of object from observer:
The object appeared
to be approximately one and one-half (12) miles to
two (2)
miles from the observer at an angle of elevation of approximately
450 from the horizon. MRS MICHEL was unable to judge the
altitude, but stated that it was relatively high.
Time in sight:
The object remained within MRS MICHEL'S
view for approximately ten (10) minutes.
Appearance of object: The object was silver in color
and luminous and had the shape of a disk and was likened to a
The size was indicated as being approximately
eight to twelve inches (8 to 12") in diameter when viewed at
the stated distance. The informant compared this to the
relative size of a small training type aircraft when viewed at
the stated distance.
Direction of flight: The object appeared to be travel-
ling from the east to a north, northwestern direction.
Tactics or maneuvers:
The object appeared to be ascend-
ing at a gradual pace. However, the object did not swerve from
its direct upward line of flight.
No tactics or maneuvers
other than these were evident. The object was turning in an
end-over-end fashion.
j.. Evidence of exhaust: No smoke, fumes or odors were
apparent.
