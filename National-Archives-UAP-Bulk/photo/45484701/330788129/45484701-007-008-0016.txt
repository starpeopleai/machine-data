SUBJECT: (Unclassified)
UNKNOWN SUBJECT (S): Reported Sighting of
Unidentified Flying Object in Vicinity of Levelland,
Texas, night of 2-3 November 1957.
TO:
Director, Special Investigations
Headquarters, USAF
Washington 25, D. C
1. Reference TWX from the Detachment Commander, OSI
Detachment 2303, Reese AFB, Texas, subject as above, dated 4
November 1957.
2
At approximately 2300 hours, 2 November 1957, the
Provost Marshal's Office, Reese AFB, Texas, advised they had
received a report that an airplane had crashed and was burning in
the vicinity of Levelland, Texas. Additional reports filtered in to
the Provost Marshal's Office by means of civil police radio indicat-
ing that an Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) was reported rather
than a burning aircraft. Major DANIEL R. KESTER, Reese AFB,
Provost Marshal, conducted a preliminary investigation and obtained
sufficient information for a TWX report in accordance with AFR 200-2.
Such report was transmitted on 3 November 1957 by Captain SAMUEL
GOLDSTEIN, Acting Counterintelligence Officer, Reese AFB, Texas.
Information obtained from both the Provost Marshal's Office
and the Wing Intelligence Office on 4 November 1957, reflected that
the names of some of the witnesses as contained in the original TWX
were misspelled. Identifying data which was not available at the
time the TWX was transmitted was made available. A corrected
list of witnesses who reportedly observed the citing is as follows:
Sheriff WEIR CLEM, Levelland, Texas
PEDRO SAUCEDO, farm hand and part-time barber,
Levelland, Texas
RONALD MARTIN, truck driver, Levelland, Texas
