SECURITY INFORMATION
At 1745 hours, 2 December 1951, the statement was obtained from CARI. HART,
Sh., which is set forth verbatim:
"On Aug 50, 1951 at about 11:30 PM took pictures of strange
objects passing overhead from North to South. I saw three separate
groups of objects.
Two pictures are of the second group.
Three are
of the third group.
The last negative has not been found and is not
in my possession.
/s/ Carl Hart Jr."
8. On 30 November 1951, WILLIAM "BILL" HAMS, Photographer for the Avalanche-
Journal Newspaper, Lubbock, Texas, was interviewed as to his knowledge of the type
of work, the authenticity of the pictures turned in by CARL HART, JR., during a
period covering approximately one (1) year. Mr. HAMS stated that he believed HART
to be the best amateur photographer in Lubbock and vicinity and that HART had
never given them any occasion to doubt his work. In referring to the pictures taken
by HART on 30 August 1951, HAM stated that the newspaper had also been doubtful as
to the authenticity of the negatives and had run exhaustive tests on the negatives
before they were published in the newspaper.
HAM further stated that these tests
were very thorough and that they had used the technical knowledge of other photog-r
grapher in the city and none could find any evidence of the negatives having been
tampered with in any way. HAM did not believe that HART would attempt to-perpetrate
a hoax as the young man had turned in several times very remarkable pictures
which work had been authentic.
9. All investigation as requested having been completed and there being no
further undeveloped leads, this case is closed.
