Q. (BY AGENT HILDRUTH) Lt KUNZMAN, I understand that on or about 30 October
1948, while on a flying mission, you sighted an unidentified flying object.
Will you state the date and time you sighted this object--giving Zonal Time?

I think it was the 3th of October 1948, but I would have to look it up
to be sure. We flew practically every day. It was some time in the after-
noon. I would say it was just about 2230 hours Zonal Time (1430).

What type of aircraft were you in, and what was the speed, altitude and
direction of flight?

We were flying an F-82-F; speed at the time was approximately 240; our
altitude was approximately 8,000 feet; our direction was Northerly.

What was your distance and direction from a city, town or known landmark?

We were ten miles West--off the coast--and about fifteen miles North of
Grays Harbor, Washington.

What was the clock position of this object from your aircraft?

At the time of the initial sighting, it was 10:00 o'clock and they moved
all the way around to 3:00 o'clock and disappeared just as they came back
