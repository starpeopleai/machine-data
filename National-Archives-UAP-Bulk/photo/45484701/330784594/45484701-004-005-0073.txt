ATIC (AFCIN-4E2)
1. The following information received from the Cleveland, Ohio Field
Office of the Federal Bureau of Investigation on 11 May 1960 is fur-
nished for your information and any action deemed appropriate.
On 5 May 1960 Mrs. Mary J. Kramer, Route 303, Richfield,
Ohio, telephone OLiver 9-5681, telephonically advised Special
Agent John L. Stanley of the Cleveland Office that on four or
five occasions during the past month between the hours of one
and four AM she had observed a moon shaped luminous object
which seemed to change colors to green, yellow, and chartreuse,
and move slowly across the sky, being in view for twenty minutes
or a half hour. She stated she could not tell how large or how
far avay the object was.
Mrs. Kramer, who is writing a book and often stays up until
early in the morning to do her writing, stated she last saw the
object about two weeks ago and that on this occasion the object
appeared to send down rays of light similar to the beam of a
flashlight. She added that she woke two of her daughters, ages
17 and 19, and that they observed the object.
Mrs. Kramer stated that she had not reported seeing the flying
object to any authorities prior to calling the FBI inasmuch as
they would think she was "a screwball."
2.
Investigation is not being conducted by the FBI in this matter.
