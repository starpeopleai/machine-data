1. SYNOPSIS: Mr. GRAY We MILLER, 716 Summit Avenue, Webster
Groves, Missouri, reported sighting two (2) objects in the sky at
approximately 1700 hours 29 May 1952 off the coast of the Florida
Keys while aboard the U.S.S, Oriskany CV-34. MILLER related that
he was standing beside the ship's telescope on the starboard side
of the navigation deck and observed the two (2) objects through
the telescope upon being informed by the Radar Officer, name unknown,
that there were two (2) saucers in the sky. Evaluation of information
cannot be determined.

2. DETAILS: On 10 June 1952, Mr. GRAY We MILLER, 716 Summit
Avenue, Webster Groves, Missouri, was interviewed by Special Agent
KENNETH L. THOMPSON at his (MILLER's) home, upon his own request.

Mr. MILLER stated that he is a Field Representative Engineer for
Vickers Electric Division, Vickers, Incorporated, 1815 Olive Street,
Ste Louis, Missouri. MILLER stated that on 29 May 1952 he was aboard
the U.S. Aircraft Carrier Oriskany, CV-34, Commanded by Captain Je O.
LAMBRECHT, U.S. Navy, enroute from Norfolk, Virginia to Guantanamo,
Cuba. At approximately 1'700 to 1730 hours, 29 May 1952, the Radar
Officer, name unknown, ran from the Radar Room and said that there
were two (2) saucers in the sky. MILLER advised that he (MILLER)

was standing beside the ship's telescope, located on the starboard
side of the navigation deck, and immediately looked at the objects

in the sky through the telescope. MILLER stated that there were two
(2) objects in the sky, at an estimated altitude of 10,000 to 15,000
feet, flying in a "front" formation. Each object was leaving a white
vapor trail.

MILLER described the objects and conditions under which
they were observed as follows: Eliptical in shape, with what appeared
