SYNOPSIS:
Mrs. EUGENE SCHUMAKER, 840 Frazier, Topeka, Kansas,
stated to news reporters on 8 July 1950 that her husband and three (3)
other men sighted an unidentified object in the air near Richland, Kansas,
at approximately 0745 hours, 7 July 1950.
The object was described as a
"flying saucer." (Source:
Topeka State Journal, Topeka, Kansas, 8 July
1950)
