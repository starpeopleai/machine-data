SYNOPSIS:
On 16 March 1950, RHEIN and WEBB observed what appeared
to be flying discs. Said objects were of a bright aluminum color, flat and
oblong in shape. The number of objects observed varied from five to fifteen
and their size from one-sixteenth to one-half an inch, if examined at arm's
length. Altitude was estimated over 20,000 feet.
Their maneuverability
was erratic and sometimes turning on their axes at pronounced angles, and
unprecedented rates of speed.

EBB, both employees at Perry Smoak Garage, Farmington, New Mexico, stated
substantially that they had observed what appeared to be a group of flying
discs. This observation took place at Farmington, New Mexico, 1000 o'clock,
16 March 1950.
