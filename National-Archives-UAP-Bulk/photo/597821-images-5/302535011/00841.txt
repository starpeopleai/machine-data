CIGAR ON FACE OF MOON

At about 5:30 A.M. (August 27), 19 year old Dan Boros re-
ported he saw a cigar-shaped craft, with lighted port-holes, cross
the face of the moon slowly, then pick up speed and vanish in the
east.

He is night watchman for Neil Greenhouses, Inc., 2045 on
Rte. 91, Gates Mills. When he made the report he sounded shaken.

‘When it started to pick up speed after it had gone across the
face of the moon, a light glowed all around it and it trailed smoke
for about five seconds,” he said. ‘'l would judge it was 50 to 80
feet long.”’

Earl Neff, of C.U.P., taped the report. Neff thought that
Boros undoubtedly saw a "mother ship which launches the saucers.”
''l think he underestimated the size. It musthave been much larger.
It was interplanetary, and we don't often get reports on them.” . . .
